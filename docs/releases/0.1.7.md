First public release of **Bases Spotlight View Expanded**, maintained by [Maru](https://marumimamori.me/).

An expanded fork of [Obsidian Bases Spotlight View](https://github.com/mymindstorm/obsidian-bases-spotlight-view) by **Brendan Early / mymindstorm**, preserving the original MIT license and copyright notice.

### Included

- Dual-pane browsing for Markdown notes, images, and PDFs in Obsidian Bases.
- Editors for Text, List, Tags, Number, Checkbox, Date, and Date & time, plus native File/Folder/Property pickers when available.
- Real multi-value chips, per-value removal, suggestions, and continuous entry after Enter.
- Live property type/value synchronization with unfinished-input preservation.
- Fields that grow with content, saved layout controls, and alignment that adapts to icon sizing.
- Darker property type icon boxes with a lighter hover state.
- Individual settings reset arrows, attachment sidecars, and Credits & License settings.

### Install With BRAT

Use **BRAT: Add a beta plugin for testing** and enter:

`https://github.com/marumimamori/bases-spotlight-view-expanded`

Enable **Bases Spotlight View Expanded**, open a Base, and choose its layout.

For manual installation, extract `bases-spotlight-view-expanded.zip` into `<Vault>/.obsidian/plugins/bases-spotlight-view-expanded/`, then reload Obsidian and enable the plugin. The release also provides `main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css` individually, alongside `LICENSE` and `NOTICE.md`.

**Requires Obsidian 1.10.0 or newer with Bases enabled.** Native property widgets depend on the installed Obsidian version. This is a beta release; reports with reproducible steps are welcome.
